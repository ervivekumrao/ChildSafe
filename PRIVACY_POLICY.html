Privacy First.

Last Updated: March 05, 2025

At ChildSafe, we believe that security should never come at the expense of privacy. Our mission is to provide parents with powerful tools while maintaining the highest standards of data protection for the entire family.

ChildSafe is built with "Privacy by Design." We do not sell your personal data, and we collect only what is strictly necessary to keep your children safe.

1. Transparent Data Collection

To enable monitoring and administrative controls, the following data is securely synced from the Child Device to the parent dashboard:

Location Intelligence

We collect background location coordinates to provide real-time updates and movement history. This allows you to verify your child's safety at any time.

Secure Communications

The app accesses SMS messages, call logs, and contacts to help you identify and prevent potentially unsafe interactions or cyberbullying.

App Analytics

We monitor app usage duration and installation lists to enforce screen time policies and allow you to manage which apps are accessible.

2. Behavioral Biometrics

Our unique Smart PIN feature analyzes entry rhythm and speed. This data is used solely to generate a unique security signature for your device and is never shared or used for identification outside of app security.

3. Administrative Rights

ChildSafe utilizes high-level Android permissions (Device Administrator & Device Owner) to ensure policies cannot be bypassed. These rights are used exclusively for:

  • Preventing unauthorized uninstallation.
  • Enforcing Kiosk Mode and app restrictions.
  • Remote device locking for screen time compliance.

4. Security & Storage

All family data is encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS in transit and stored in secure, compartmentalized databases provided by Google Firebase. Access is strictly limited to the authenticated parent account.

5. Your Data, Your Control

You have the absolute right to access, export, or delete your family's data. Account deletion can be performed instantly via the app settings, which permanently wipes all associated cloud records.
