Comprehensive Setup Guide

Follow these detailed steps to ensure full administrative control.

1

Command Center Activation

Install ChildSafe on your primary (Parent) device. Sign up for a new account and verify your email.

  • On the User Type screen, select Parent.
  • Complete your profile setup, including your name and optional profile picture.
2

The "Secret Scanner" Mode

Start with a new or factory-reset child device. This is essential for granting "Device Owner" status.

  • On the first "Welcome" screen, tap six times in the same spot (outside of buttons).
  • A hidden QR code scanner will launch. Ensure the device has a stable internet connection.
3

Secure Provisioning

Open your Parent Dashboard and tap the + button to reveal your unique enrollment QR code.

  • Scan the QR code with the child's device. Follow the prompts to connect to Wi-Fi.
  • The system will automatically download and provision the management profile.
4

Identity & Enrollment

Launch ChildSafe on the child's device and sign in with your parent account credentials.

  • Select Kid as the user type and enter the child's name.
  • Complete the on-screen permission prompts to finalize the link to your dashboard.
