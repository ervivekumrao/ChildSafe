Child Dashboard

Your central hub for monitoring a specific child device.

📊

Live Insights

  • Quick Stats: Instant view of account verification and linked devices.
  • Usage Reports: Detailed breakdown of app screen time (Daily/Weekly/Monthly).
  • Device Health: Live battery percentage and connectivity status tracking.
📡

Advanced Tracking

  • Real-time Locate: Pinpoint exact coordinates with high-precision radar.
  • Periodic Routes: Automated snapshots of movements over selected durations (15m to 24h).
  • Location History: View historical paths and timestamps of where the device has been.
💬

Safety Logs

  • SMS Monitoring: View incoming and outgoing text messages remotely.
  • Call History: Access detailed call logs with timestamps and contact names.
  • Contact List: Browse the child's full address book to ensure safe social circles.

Usage & App Control

Granular control over how and when apps are used.

📱

Application Management

  • Remote Blocking: Instantly hide apps from the launcher or suspend their execution.
  • App Permissions: Review and manage permissions granted to individual applications.
  • Kiosk Inclusion: Choose which apps are allowed during focused study or play sessions.
⏳

Screen Time Limits

  • Individual App Limits: Set daily time quotas for specific time-sink apps.
  • Weekly Scheduling: Configure different limits for weekdays and weekends.
  • Automatic Lock: Apps become unusable once their daily quota is reached.
🔒

Device Restrictions

  • Smart Locking: Set global device lock schedules to ensure healthy sleep routines.
  • Emergency Bypass: Critical features like emergency calls remain always available.
  • Remote Commands: Force lock, unlock, or reboot the device instantly from your dashboard.

Ethical Commitment
